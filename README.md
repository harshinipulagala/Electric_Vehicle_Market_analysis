# 🚗 Electric Vehicle Market Analysis


📌 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the **Electric Vehicle (EV) market in the United States** using a **Tableau-based interactive dashboard**. The dashboard highlights market growth trends, state-wise EV adoption, manufacturer performance, and the relationship between price and electric range. It is designed to help stakeholders, analysts, and EV researchers understand market behavior, consumer adoption, and product performance over the years.

---

 🧩 Dataset

**Source:** [Kaggle EV Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/nuhmanpk/electric-vehicle-population-data-2025)  

**Key Fields:**  
- Model Year  
- Make  
- Model  
- Electric Vehicle Type (BEV/PHEV)  
- CAFV Eligibility  
- Range  
- State  
- Price  

**Records:** 247,336 electric vehicle registrations  

---

🔍 Key Insights

- 🚀 EV adoption increased rapidly after 2016, reaching its highest point in 2023  
- 🌎 Washington leads the nation with the highest EV count (~246K)  
- 🏆 Tesla is the dominant manufacturer, holding over 50% of total EV registrations  
- 🔋 Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) have significantly higher electric range compared to Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)  
- 💰 Premium EVs cost more but sometimes offer lower range compared to mid-range models  
- 🌱 Overall trend shows growing preference for BEVs due to better range and performance

📊 Dashboard Highlights

**Metrics Displayed:**  
- Total EVs  
- Total BEV & PHEV Count  
- % Share of BEV vs PHEV  
- Average Price  
- Average Electric Range  

**Visualizations Included:**  
- EV Growth Trend by Model Year  
- EV Distribution Across U.S. States (Map View)  
- Top 10 Manufacturers by EV Count  
- Range vs Price Analysis segmented by price category  
- Average Range by Vehicle Type (BEV vs PHEV)  

**Filters and Parameters:**  
- Model Year Range  
- State Selection  
- Electric Vehicle Type  
- Top N Manufacturers (Dynamic parameter)

 ✅ Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that the **U.S. EV market** is experiencing strong growth, driven primarily by BEVs and led by manufacturers like Tesla. Consumer interest in EVs continues to increase, supported by advancements in range, falling prices, and supportive state policies. This dashboard can help businesses, policymakers, and analysts make informed decisions regarding **EV adoption, market opportunities, and investment strategies**.
